fostering innovation in energy-efficient AI development. By embedding energy efficiency

Lastly, the impact AI can have on water demand and usage and energy affordability must
be carefully managed through sustainable practices and responsible policies. Given that
AI requires significant computational power and that data centers can consume large
amounts of electricity, energy costs for consumers, particularly those in areas with
significant AI infrastructure, may face increased energy costs. Energy costs in California
have increased significantly over the past several years 2, outpacing the rate of inflation
by some measures. Additionally, data centers for cooling purposes also consume
significant amounts of water. Utilizing water-conscious cooling solutions, including air-
based cooling mechanisms, can help reduce the dependence on water and decrease the
strain on water resources. Overall, avoiding unnecessary or wasteful AI applications and
user education could be solutions to the potential problems of grid reliability, water
demand, and energy affordability.
