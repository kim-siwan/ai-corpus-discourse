RE: Comments on the Development of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Action Plan

Dear Mr. D’Souza,

Thank you for the opportunity to review and comment on the Development of an Artificial Intelligence (AI) Action Plan directed by
Presidential Executive Order 14179. First and foremost, Scott County supports the development of a clear plan for guiding AI’s
significant potential to increase the efficiency and efficacy of government services. As the local entity tasked with delivering programs and
services mandated at the state and federal level, we have a deep understanding of the challenges posed by the complexity of these
systems, which also gives us a clear perspective on the opportunities for innovation and efficiency.

As the level of government closest to the people, counties play a critical role in delivering essential services that range from social
programs and public safety to infrastructure and emergency response. AI presents a truly transformational opportunity for all levels of
government to work together to enhance service delivery, streamline operations, and make data-driven decisions that directly impact
residents' daily lives. By leveraging AI to integrate data across departments—such as linking social services with public safety to identify
at-risk individuals or using predictive analytics for infrastructure maintenance—counties can create more efficient, responsive, and cost-
effective programs. However, AI deployment must be guided by principles of transparency and accountability. Counties need clear
governance frameworks to ensure AI protect sensitive data and maintaining public trust.

Counties also bring a unique perspective to AI implementation, as they must navigate complex funding structures, intergovernmental
partnerships, and myriad community needs. Workforce training, responsible procurement, and stringent data privacy protections must be
prioritized to ensure AI benefits all residents equitably. Additionally, policies should support counties in responsibly integrating AI into
existing programs while safeguarding against risks such as cybersecurity threats and algorithmic discrimination.

We appreciate the opportunity to submit these comments, and we hope you will give them consideration in your efforts to develop an AI
Action Plan that encourages innovation and efficiency across all levels of government.

Respectfully,

Lezlie Vermillion
Scott County Administrator
