 General Comment
Big tech companies should NOT profit from the hard work of individual artists around the globe without providing these artists with
monetary compensation for their contributions. Companies using any creative media to train AI models should be REQUIRED to (1)
obtain explicit, written consent from artists before using their work in AI training, and (2) pay royalties to the original creators for the use
of their work in both AI training and content generation.

As a company working closely with many talented artists, we understand firsthand that creating artistic media requires dedication, skill,
and genuine effort. We firmly believe that the creative talents of artists everywhere deserve respect and recognition. Our mission is to
empower INDIVIDUALS, whose creativity and imagination make entertainment possible. We would consider it a failure on our part if
we allowed large corporations to overshadow the beauty and uniqueness of individual creation. There is inherent value in the art created
by individuals around the world, and it’s time these artists receive the recognition and compensation they rightfully deserve.

Empowering creativity together,
Harrison Thorne
Founder, Musicaloft LLC
