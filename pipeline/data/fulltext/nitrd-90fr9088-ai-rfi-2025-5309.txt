 General Comment
The generic approval for materials collection and carte blanche provided to AI companies is a scourge on the long term meaningful
development of most any other technology or materials.

Allowing things like unrestricted and unapproved collection of our materials for training represents considerable harm to Loam LLC's
business prospects working in the game and software development space, and has and will result in the theft of our materials without the
consent of the company or any authors. When Loam uses music, materials, content, we are required to adhere to modern copyright laws
and appropriately compensate third parties for work they do related to our games and applications, as outlined by those third parties. This
policy demonstrates not only is a blatant disregard for the efforts and value put into the products we create, but damage to the very
materials that could be used to improve AI in the first place if we were consulted and asked to opt in to collection and training.

Biden-era policies on AI should be maintained to ensure meaningful technical developments in all spaces, not just a muted growth only in
AI that crushes other industries, and by extension its own training data. If these AI tools are actually worth the trillions these companies
allege, surely they can appropriately compensate and contribute back to the economy to provide mutual growth without requiring special
treatment.
